SPECIFICATION DOCUMENTS AND SCHEMAS
-----------------------------------
The IHO and IALA specification publications consulted while building the
validator, the S-201 Feature Catalogue XML, the IHO S-100 / S-201 schema
families with the OGC, ISO and W3C schemas they import, the S-158 check tables,
the S-62 producer-code data and the plain-text extracts derived from the
publications are NOT redistributed in the public repository. All of them are
freely available from their official sources; the file
dev/spec-sources/MANIFEST.md lists every one (name, size, SHA-256, where to
obtain it) so a local working copy can be assembled that matches what the
validator's citations were checked against.
